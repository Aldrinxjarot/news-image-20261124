# Story list — Tuesday, 24 November 2026

Every story was checked against wire-style coverage dated Tuesday
24 November 2026 before it was drawn. Where an event itself belongs
to an earlier day, the page either says that day in words or the
item was dropped.

## Used on the page

1. **HERO — Nvidia's Huang bounce fades (Tuesday trading).**
   Reuters' "Trading Day" wrap, dated 24 Nov 2026: "The excitement
   around Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia faded quickly on Tuesday,
   with shares sliding 6%"; the same wrap's key moves give Nasdaq
   −1.5%, S&P 500 −0.5%, Dow +0.9% — the four bars in the page's
   "Tuesday's close" chart. Reuters also reported Tuesday that
   Congress is preparing to question Huang. The backstory is labelled
   on the page: Huang resigned on 12 November (CFR; USA Today) and
   was reinstated as chief executive and chair after President Trump
   intervened (Principia and The Daily Edition, 24 Nov editions;
   ar-es.eu, Tue 24 Nov). Tuesday's news — the rally fading and the
   stock's 6% slide — is the hero; no Monday figure is presented as
   Tuesday's.
2. **Huawei convicted (Reuters, Tuesday).** Reuters, 24 Nov:
   "China's Huawei was convicted on Tuesday in a sprawling US
   criminal case on charges the technology and telecommunications
   giant deceived banks about its business in Iran and stole trade
   secrets from US companies."
3. **Sanofi buys Insmed for $41bn (announced Tuesday).** Sanofi's
   own press release, "Paris, November 24, 2026": an agreement to
   acquire Insmed for $175 per share in cash, total equity value
   about $41.0 billion / €37.6 billion, including Brinsupri and
   Arikayce and the phase 3 TPIP programme. WSJ (Tuesday): the
   biggest pharmaceutical transaction of the year.
4. **Israel — Eisenkot's first coalition pact (Reuters, Tuesday).**
   Reuters, 24 Nov: Yashar, led by former military chief Gadi
   Eisenkot, "said on Tuesday it agreed to sign a coalition pact"
   with Avigdor Lieberman's Israel Our Home — the first such
   alliance since the 27 October election stripped Netanyahu of his
   majority; Eisenkot has until 6 December to form a government.
5. **Ukraine — overnight strike on Zaporizhzhia (reported Tuesday).**
   Just Security's Early Edition of 24 Nov 2026, citing AP:
   Zaporizhzhia regional governor Mykhailo Semikin said an overnight
   Russian attack killed two people in the city and wounded another
   40, including four children, striking an apartment building.
   The page says "overnight" and "dozens" rather than a precise
   wounded count, because a second Tuesday report put the wounded
   higher.
